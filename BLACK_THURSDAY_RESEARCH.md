# Black Thursday: Mission 115 order of battle and formation research

Eighth Air Force Mission 115, Schweinfurt, 14 October 1943. Researched 2026-10-28. Machine-readable data is in `historical_data/black_thursday/` (`bomb_groups.json`, `formations.json`, `sources.json`). Confidence levels: VERIFIED / PROBABLE / UNVERIFIED / UNKNOWN. ESTIMATE means a value made up for the game, with no direct source.

## Confirmed facts (VERIFIED)

- **Totals.** 291 B-17s were dispatched: 149 from the 1st Bomb Division and 142 from the 3rd (S1, S3, S6). 60 were lost over the Continent or the sea (S3). On top of that, 17 suffered major damage (S3), and the 1st Division had 5 crash, crash-land or be abandoned over England (S6).
  - The 1st Division lost 45; 48 of its aircraft failed to bomb, leaving 101 attackers (S6).
  - The 3rd Division lost 15; 14 failed to bomb, leaving 128 attackers (S6).
  - The 1st Division is the weak point: about a third of its aircraft aborted or fell out before the target.
- **Groups.** The groups were exactly those in the brief.
  - 1st Division: 91st, 92nd, 303rd, 305th, 306th, 351st, 379th, 381st, 384th.
  - 3rd Division: 94th, 95th, 96th, 100th, 385th, 388th, 390th.
- **2nd Division (B-24s).** The B-24s failed to assemble in the weather. 29 aircraft from two units flew a diversion toward Emden/Frisia instead of the planned southern route to Schweinfurt (S3; S7 gives 60 scheduled and 21 flying the feint).
- **1st Division wings as briefed** (S1, S2; dispatch counts from S1):

| Wing (order) | Leader | Lead | High | Low | Dispatched |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40th CW, lead wing | Col. Peaslee (with the 92nd) | 92nd (19) | 306th (18) | 305th (16) | 53 |
| 1st CW, second wing | Lt Col Milton | 91st (11) | 351st (S2) / 381st (S1) | 381st (S2) / 351st (S1) | 44 |
| 41st CBW, third wing | Col. Rohr | 379th (17) | 303rd (19) | 384th (16) | 52 |

- **1st CW altitudes (S2).** Briefed lead 23,000 ft and high 24,000 ft. Oxygen altitude was 24,000 ft. The force descended to 21,000 ft after the target.
- **351st bomb run** (S2, primary record):
  - Initial point (IP) at Würzburg; turned on the IP at 14:38; bombs away at 14:41.
  - 23,550 ft true; CIAS 150 mph, TAS 222 mph.
  - Axis of attack 58° true, true heading 64°, wind 220°/30 mph.
  - 5° S-turns on the run-in, then 40–60 s straight and level.
  - The group bombed as a unit: the leader sighted range and deflection and the deputy sighted range.
  - On the run the group "took the normal trail behind the 91st and 381st Groups".
  - Rally was a right turn to 130°.
- **1st Division route (S2 flak report and J-form).**
  - Out: Orfordness at 12:33 at 21,000 ft, then West Schouwen, Turnhout and Düren.
  - Right turn at Bad Nauheim, then an S-turn from Lohr and Karlstadt to the Würzburg IP and Schweinfurt.
  - Home: Ebrach, then on toward Bitche, crossing out at Bourg d'Ault at 16:43 at 21,000 ft and reaching Beachy Head at 17:03 at 10,000 ft.
- **Losses per 1st Division group.** These sum to 45 and are confirmed by MACR clusters (S11) and by Craven & Cate's 29 lost for the 40th CW as briefed (S3):
  - 91st 1, 92nd 6, 303rd 1, 305th 13, 306th 10
  - 351st 1, 379th 6, 381st 1, 384th 6
- **The 305th** dispatched 16 and had 1 abort. 12 were shot down before the target and 1 after it; 2 returned (S6).
- **The 306th** dispatched 18 and had 3 aborts. 10 were lost and 5 returned (S10, S6, S13).
- **The 100th** had only 8 crews. They flew as two four-ship flights, one attached to the 95th (filling its high squadron) and one to the 390th, inside the 13th CBW (S8).
- **Escort plan** (S2, S3):
  - One P-47 group per task force from the Dutch coast (51°30'N 04°15'E) to the limit of its range.
  - One P-47 group per task force for withdrawal.
  - Four squadrons of Spitfire IX to mid-Channel, plus two squadrons of Spitfire IX sweeping for stragglers.
  - A P-38 sweep was planned, but the P-38s were not operational until the next day.
  - The P-47s turned back near Aachen (S3, S12), at about 13:30–13:33 (S2 S-2 report; S24).
  - Weather prevented the withdrawal escort (S3; S1 personal account; S2 "no fighter escort home").

## Probable or approximate

- **Division separation.** Craven & Cate say the two divisions were to "cross enemy defenses abreast, some 30 miles apart" (S3). The 351st briefing said "3rd ATF same target 30 minutes behind us" (S2). Actual bombing times were 14:39–14:45 for the 1st Division and 14:51–14:57 for the 3rd, a gap of about 12 minutes (S9, corroborated by 351st 14:41 and 100th 14:54). The 1st Division was about 13 minutes late (S2).
- **3rd Division dog-leg.** After the escort left near Eupen, the 3rd Division turned south toward Trier and the Mosel, then east to its IP (S16; the shape is visible on the S5 map).
- **3rd Division wings.**
  - 45th CBW: 96th and 388th.
  - 4th CBW: 94th and 385th.
  - 13th CBW: 95th and 390th, plus the 100th flights.
  - The 96th and 45th are said to have led the division (S22 [citation needed], S16, S23 with Col. Archie Old leading).
- **3rd Division losses (S11 MACRs).** 94th 6 (including Brennan's Circus, ditched), 96th 6, 95th 1, 390th 1, and 0 for the 100th, 385th and 388th. That totals 14 against the reported 15. One loss is unassigned; it is possibly the 96th's 7th.
- **The 305th as composite.** The 305th could not find the 40th CW and joined the 1st CW (S7), filling the 1st CW's empty low slot after radio contact. The 381st, assigned as low group, attached itself to the high group, the 351st (S15, excerpt only).
- **Group box sizes.** The 303rd's own chart shows 20 aircraft: lead squadron 6, the other two squadrons 7 each in 3-3-1 rows (S1). The 21-plane box (Jan–Sep 1943) and 18-plane box were the doctrine (S4, S18). The 36-plane box of October 1943 was **not** used this day.
- **Doctrine stacking** (Craven & Cate summer-1943 diagram, S4):
  - Within a group: lead squadron 25,000 ft, high squadron +100 ft, low squadron −250 ft; 750 ft from top aircraft to bottom.
  - Within a wing: lead group 25,000 ft, high group +1,000 ft, low group −1,000 ft; 3,000 ft overall.
  - Freeman gives a wing box of about 3,000 ft vertical, 7,000 ft deep and 2,000 ft wide (S18).
- **Luftwaffe tactics** (S3, S2, S1, S12):
  - Single-engine Fw 190 and Bf 109 fighters made frontal and stern passes.
  - Waves of twin-engine Bf 110, Me 210/410, Ju 88 and Do 217 aircraft fired 21 cm rockets from about 1,000 yd astern, heaviest at the IP and on the bomb run. The 351st lost 42-6096 to a rocket.
  - The Germans concentrated on one formation at a time, then finished off stragglers.
  - Fighters refuelled and attacked again on the way home.
  - German records show 38 fighters destroyed and 20 damaged in combat (S3).

## Uncertainties and disagreements

1. **The 1st CW high and low groups.** The 303rd's table (S1) has the 381st high and the 351st low. The 351st's own briefing and flak report (S2) have the 351st high and the 381st low. I used S2.
2. **Dispatch counts.** S1 gives the 351st 16 and the 303rd 19, but their own reports give 18 and 20. With the group-report values, the 1st Division per-group sum is 152, not 149. The 149 total (S1, S3, S6) is VERIFIED; the per-group split has about ±3 of uncertainty. S21 has the 92nd sending 22 and losing 2 (outlier). S26 has the 384th with 18 taking off (S1 says 16).
3. **Which wing actually led the 1st Division.** The briefed order was 40th, 1st, 41st (S2). HistoryNet says the 40th "tagged along with the 41st" (S7). The 351st's group leader praised the 1st CW leader for "leading the Air Division" (S2). Craven & Cate still say the 40th led (S3). This is unresolved.
4. **The 40th CW count.** Craven & Cate give the 40th CW "seven of its forty-nine" lost before the target and 29 in total (S3). This counts the 305th inside the 40th even though it flew with the 1st CW.
5. **306th losses.** HistoryNet says 12; every other source says 10.
6. **Total aircraft lost.** Sources give 60 (shot down), 65 (60 plus 5 crashed in England, S1, S6) or 77 (60 plus 17 written off, Wikipedia). German losses are given as 38 destroyed and 20 damaged (S3) or 31 destroyed and 12 written off (S12).
7. **Attacking total.** 229 (S9, S24, S6) or 228 (S3).
8. **3rd Division order of wings and positions within wings.** Not found. One unidentified excerpt had the 13th CW forming first.
9. **Per-group dispatch counts for the 3rd Division.** Not found, apart from the 100th's 8 (S8). Only the 142 total is VERIFIED.
10. **Escort fighter groups.** The 56th and 353rd made contact; the 4th FG missed the rendezvous; the 352nd covered the B-24s (S7, a single source). S25 confirms the 353rd was on the escort. Wikipedia's "24 Spitfire squadrons" is unsupported, and S3 and S2 list only 4+2 squadrons of Spitfire IX.

## Escort

- **P-47s.** The 56th and 353rd FG met the 1st Division force and escorted it to about Aachen. The 3rd Division picked up P-47s at the Belgian coast and lost them near Eupen. The 4th FG failed to rendezvous. The 352nd covered the B-24 feint (S7, S16).
- **Range limit.** The P-47s had no drop tanks, which is why they turned back near Aachen (S9, S3).
- **Withdrawal cover.** The planned withdrawal P-47 group and the Spitfire IX squadrons were largely weathered out (S3, S2).
- **P-38s.** The planned P-38 sweep did not fly (S3).
- **Coast.** RAF Spitfires were planned for the Channel legs only.

## What the game should simplify

- **Composition.** Use the briefed wing structure. Size groups from `bomb_groups.json`, scaling to the 291 total. Fill the 3rd Division groups with roughly equal shares of 142 (about 20 each, the 100th 8) and flag those as estimates.
- **Formation shape.** Use one rigid group template (lead, high and low squadrons of 6–7) and one wing template (±305 m vertical). Treat lateral and longitudinal offsets as tunable values; they are not historical facts.
- **Divisions.** Model the two divisions as two parallel streams: 3rd Division about 48 km south, with an optional dog-leg after Eupen, and about 12 minutes behind at the target.
- **Abort attrition.** Model it explicitly. The 1st Division lost about a third of its strength before the target to aborts and shoot-downs, so formations should thin out and close up.
- **Bomb run.** Switch from wing formation to groups in trail from the IP. Bomb on the leader, then turn right to rally.
- **305th variant.** Offer an optional "as flown" scenario with the 305th attached to the 1st CW as its low group.


## How the game uses this (implementation decisions, 28 Oct 2026)

- **Where it lives:**
  - `src/oob.js` holds the force and its layout: `FORCE` (divisions, wings, groups, aircraft counts) and `LAYOUT` (every offset and interval in one place).
  - `src/history.js` reads the group records.
  - `scripts/history/merge.mjs` builds the merged database files and `RESEARCH_PROGRESS.md`.
  - `tests/blackthursday.test.mjs` checks both.
- **Aircraft counts:**
  - **1st Division (149):** from its dispatch table: 92nd 19, 306th 18, 305th 16, 91st 11, 351st 16, 381st 17, 379th 17, 303rd 19, 384th 16. The 351st's and 303rd's own reports say 18 and 20 (152 in all). The table is used so the division adds up to its verified 149.
  - **3rd Division (142):**
    - From the group records: 94th 21, 95th 18, 96th 41 (flown as the 96-A and 96-B groups), 100th 8 (two four-ship flights, one with the 95th and one with the 390th), and 385th 22 (from its association's sortie list).
    - **ESTIMATE:** the remaining 32 are split 16 each between the 388th and the 390th. Their own sources say 16–22 and 15–18.
- **Wing positions:**
  - **1st Division:** as briefed (40th, 1st, 41st; lead, high, low as in `bomb_groups.json`, following the 351st's own version where sources differ). The 305th is placed as the 40th CBW's low group, as briefed, although it may have flown with the 1st CBW.
  - **3rd Division:**
    - 45th CBW: 96-A lead (PROBABLE), 96-B high (ESTIMATE), 388th low (the 388th memoir).
    - 4th CBW: 94th lead, 385th high (ESTIMATE).
    - 13th CBW: 95th high and 390th low (the 100th's interrogation), with the lead slot empty.
- **Spacing:**
  - **Element, squadron and group:** from `formations.json`.
  - **Compressed for the game (the default):** the historical distances put all but your own wing out of sight, so the game flies the same order of battle with the distances between the wings and the divisions shrunk (`COMPACT_LAYOUT` in `src/oob.js`). Every group, squadron, element and charted slot is unchanged.
    - **Combat wings:** about 1 km apart in trail, each stepped a little down and out.
    - **3rd Division:** close alongside to the right, 1.25 km over, a little back and below.
    - **Wing:** high and low groups ±230 m to the side and ±190 m up and down (historically ±250 m and ±305 m).
    - **Result:** the whole force flies in about 2.2 × 0.75 × 3.3 km, with groups at least 186 m apart. The fighters and the flak fight all of it for real, not only the part round you.
  - **Historical (`?spacing=historical`, kept in `HISTORICAL_LAYOUT`):** the combat wings 120 s apart (9 km at the game's 75 m/s), and the divisions abreast 48.3 km apart, which puts the 3rd Division out of sight as it historically was. Groups beyond 1.5 km are fought in the abstract (`src/streambattle.js`); the far groups still bomb when they reach the target, and their bombs are scored but not drawn.
  - **Your wing's rally:** waits only for your own combat wing.
- **Formation behaviour:**
  - **Station-keeping:** every bomber keeps station with its own slow error, a few metres over 25–70 s, banking into its corrections.
  - **Damaged bombers:** they lose power, drop back and down, and straggle.
  - **Gaps:** they stay open. Only command passes on: a lost element or group leader is replaced by his own wingman, or the nearest man of his squadron. Remnants of a group cut below four join another group of their wing.
- **Identities:**
  - **Slots:** each group's records fill its slots, with the record that says it led the group in the lead slot. Records of ground spares that did not fly, and a sortie filed under two groups, are left out. Which record flies which other slot is the game's choice, and the in-game panel (I) says the place is an estimate.
  - **Unknowns:** a bomber with no record left for it has no serial or name, and its crew are placeholders, labelled as placeholders.
- **Historical outcomes (an Option, off by default):** the records' fates weight the enemy's fire (lost 8×, written off 5×, returned damaged 0.5×, bombed and aborted 0.1×), and recorded aborts turn back early on. How each loss happened is not staged: mostly it isn't known. The default is the dynamic battle.
- **Escort:** P-47s only, turning back about 75 s after the fighting starts, near Aachen. No Spitfires, because the withdrawal cover was weathered out, and no P-51s.
- **Markings** (`src/markings.js`), painted on the detailed models near you:
  - **Group symbol on the fin:** the 1st Bomb Wing's triangle or the 4th's square with the group's letter, in white.
  - **Tail number on the fin:** in yellow, the serial without the first figure of its year.
  - **Squadron code and aircraft letter:** on the fuselage in light grey.
  - **Nickname:** in plain lettering on the nose.
  - **Rules:** only what the record has is painted; the nose art itself isn't drawn (not documented).
  - **Simplification:** on this model the star sits close behind the wing, so the code and letter both go aft of the waist window (code forward, letter aft), not either side of the star.
- **Charted slots** (`historical_data/black_thursday/slots.json`, 48 aircraft):
  - **Sources:** the 303rd's assembly chart, the 306th's chart for the day, and the 351st's Combat Report Form diagram, plus the 384th's group and high-squadron leads.
  - **Placement:** each aircraft goes into exactly its charted slot. Each placement carries its basis and confidence: where the chart gives only "element" or a side, the exact place is marked an estimate, and where the chart and an account disagree (the 303rd's Hullar and Ness), the account is used and the disagreement noted.
  - **The 303rd:** flies its chart's squadrons (lead 6, high 6, low 7). Its 20th sortie, an abort, isn't placed, because the division table gives the group 19.
- **The rally turn:** right from the bomb run's 58° onto 130° (the 351st's briefing), at about 1.2° a second (about a minute), every bomber banked about 12°, starting 45 s after your wing's bombs are away.
  - **How it's done:** the game is played in the formation's frame, so the ground, the clouds, the target, the sky and the sun swing round the formation, and the ground keeps streaming straight aft, only turned.
  - **Not flown:** the other turns (the route's S-turns, the 3rd Division's dog-leg), and the wings behind turning at their own rally. The whole force turns together.
- **Spoken names:**
  - **Records:** 1,520 surnames from the records, each recorded once in the co-pilot's voice.
  - **Hit and killed:** the co-pilot calls the station, then the name ("Tail gunner's hit!", then "Hendry."; "We lost our tail gunner.", then "Hendry."). If the co-pilot is down, the pilot calls the station only.
  - **Other ships:** "They're going for X's ship!" is recorded for each of the 182 real pilots.
  - **Placeholders:** placeholder crews keep their own recorded names.
