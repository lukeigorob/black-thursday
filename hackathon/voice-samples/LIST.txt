VOICE SAMPLES: 17 voices x 3 emotion levels x 2 lines = 102 clips, all through the game's radio sound

File names: <voice>__<emotion level>__<line>.wav
  Lines:    bandits = "Bandits, three o'clock high! There's so many of them!"
            flak    = "Pilot to crew, flak ahead, brace yourselves!"
  Emotion:  1-strong   (exaggeration 0.9: tense but steady)
            2-stronger (exaggeration 1.3: shouting, urgent)
            3-max      (exaggeration 1.8: the most emotion the model can give; can wobble or slur)

VOICES (all American unless marked British)
  chatterbox_default    Chatterbox's own built-in voice
  am_adam               plain, mid
  am_echo               lighter, younger
  am_eric               clear, mid-high
  am_fenrir             gruff, rough edge
  am_liam               young, bright
  am_michael            deep, steady
  am_onyx               deepest, slow
  am_puck               fast, energetic, high
  am_santa              old, warm, deep
  am_puck+am_echo       blend (was the ball turret)
  am_fenrir+am_eric     blend (was the right waist gunner)
  am_michael+am_onyx    blend (was the lead ship)
  bm_daniel             British, calm
  bm_fable              British, lively
  bm_george             British, older, deep
  bm_lewis              British, mid

Crew to pick for: pilot, co-pilot, navigator, bombardier, top turret, ball turret,
left waist, right waist, tail gunner, lead ship.
The same voice can go to more than one man, and each man can have his own emotion level.
